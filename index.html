How It Works

Four steps, repeated until you are caught up.

1

Identify

List every missed lesson and unfinished task. Nothing is too small to write down.

2

Prioritize

Rank items by deadline and importance. Do the urgent ones first.

3

Recover

Follow a realistic schedule of short study blocks and check items off.

4

Adjust

Review your progress at the end of the week and change the plan if needed.

Study Tips

Weekly catchup checklist

  • List all unfinished tasks and missed lessons
  • Write the deadline next to each item
  • Pick the top three for this week
  • Schedule each one in a specific time
  • Review progress at the end of the weekend

Tips for staying on track

  • Study in blocks of 25 to 45 minutes with short breaks
  • Start with the smallest step if you feel stuck
  • Plan fewer tasks than you think you can do
  • Ask your teacher about extensions early, not at the deadline
  • Turn off notifications while studying

About CatchUp Protocol

CatchUp Protocol is a proposed learning recovery service for students who are falling behind or struggling with piled-up requirements. It supports learners at different educational levels, as well as independent learners who want to organize their own study.

Research on procrastination and self-regulated learning points to the value of good planning. Many tools help you list tasks. CatchUp Protocol focuses on the harder part: deciding what to do first after you have already fallen behind.

It encourages realistic plans, steady effort, and manageable tasks. It does not promise instant grade improvement and does not replace teachers or classroom instruction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is CatchUP Protocol for?

Junior high, senior high, and college students, plus independent learners who want help organizing their study.

Will my grades improve right away?

No. The service helps you plan and stay consistent, but results depend on your effor over time.

Does the website make a schedule for me automatically?

Not in this first version. Plans are written manually, and the site does not save your progress or require an account.

Does it cost anything?

The site does not include online payments. Sample plans and resource links are free to view.
